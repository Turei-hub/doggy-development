Common questions

How do you get into my house?

Most clients give me a spare key or a lockbox code, stored under a client number rather than an address. I can also work with a smart lock if you'd rather issue a code you control.

What happens if it's pouring down?

I walk in most Auckland weather — that's what towels are for. If there's a storm warning or lightning, I swap the walk for an indoor enrichment visit at no extra charge, or move it to another day that week.

Is my dog off-lead?

Only with your written permission, only where the council allows it, and only once I've seen a reliable recall on a long line. Everything else stays on-lead.

What if my dog doesn't get on with others?

Then the group walk isn't the right fit, and that's fine. Solo walks exist for exactly this — reactive, nervous, elderly and recovering dogs all get their own route and pace.

How do I cancel a walk?

Text before 7pm the night before and there's no charge. Same-day cancellations are charged at half rate, since the slot can't usually be refilled.

About

Walks run by someone who knows your dog's name

I'm Turei. I grew up in Gisborne, on the kind of place where the dogs worked for a living — sheep dogs that could read a paddock better than most people read a room, and pig dogs that were all business right up until they weren't.

I was on a horse about as early as I could walk, and I can't remember a day of my childhood without a dog somewhere nearby. That's the whole reason I do this. I didn't decide to become a dog person. I never learned any other way to live.

Working dogs teach you to read what an animal is telling you — when it's had enough, when it's nervous and covering for it, when it wants the long way home. A kelpie in a Milford backyard isn't so different from one on a hill outside Gisborne. Both of them just want a job to do.

I also build software, which is why this site exists and why you get a photo and a note after every walk instead of wondering how it went. It means the booking side works properly, and that when something breaks I fix it myself the same day.
